About AirPay PG

AirPay PG is a payment gateway solution launched in 2024 to help businesses accept payments across multiple channels, including QRIS, e-wallets, paylater, virtual accounts, direct debit and credit/debit cards.

AirPay PG provides a single, seamless end-to-end payment integration, enabling merchants to access multiple payment services quickly and with minimal complexity.

AirPay also provides a hosted payment page capability, allowing businesses to accept payments without the need to build and maintain their own.

Backed by a fully licensed payment service provider operating across Southeast Asia, AirPay PG delivers trusted, compliant, and scalable payment solutions for businesses.
